# The forest level's assets

- **Art** (`tiles.png`, `sky.png`, `trees.png`, and `hero.png`, `boar.png`, `bee.png`,
  `snail.png`, `coin.png`, `key.png`, `chest*.png`, `gate.png` made from them): "Legacy Fantasy -
  High Forest" by Anokolisa. The character's and mobs' animation sheets are combined into one
  sheet each; the sky is mirrored into a wider strip; the items are cut from the tileset.
- **Sounds** (`sounds/`): from the "GameSounds" collection, renamed: `jump.ogg` "SWIPE Whoosh 01",
  `swing.ogg` "SWIPE Whoosh 03", `hit.ogg` "SWIPE Slash Splat 01", `defeat.ogg` "SPLAT Squelch
  03", `hurt.ogg` "VOCAL CUTE Distress Pain 03", `coin.ogg` "COINS Collect Chime 01", `stomp.ogg`
  "SPLAT Short 01", `key.ogg` "SUCCESS PICKUP Collect Beep 05", `gate.ogg` "SQUEAK Creaking 01",
  `win.ogg` "SUCCESS CHEERS Win Cute Vocal Chime 01".

Neither pack came with a license file. Check their terms before redistributing them.

`forest.ldtk` and the copies here were made by the editor's `tools/demo/make_forest.py`;
since then the level is meant to be edited in LDtk.
