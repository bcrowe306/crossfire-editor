# The forest level's assets

- **Art** (`tiles.png`, `sky.png`, `trees.png`, `snail.png`, and `hero.png`, `coin.png`,
  `key.png`, `chest*.png`, `gate.png` made from them): "Legacy Fantasy - High Forest" by
  Anokolisa. `hero.png` combines the character's idle, run and jump sheets into one sheet;
  the items are cut from the tileset.
- **Sounds** (`sounds/`): from the "GameSounds" collection, renamed (`jump.ogg` was
  "SWIPE Whoosh 01", `coin.ogg` "COINS Collect Chime 01", `stomp.ogg` "SPLAT Short 01",
  `key.ogg` "SUCCESS PICKUP Collect Beep 05", `gate.ogg` "SQUEAK Creaking 01", `win.ogg`
  "SUCCESS CHEERS Win Cute Vocal Chime 01").

Neither pack came with a license file. Check their terms before redistributing them.

`forest.ldtk` and the copies here were made by the editor's `tools/demo/make_forest.py`;
since then the level is meant to be edited in LDtk.
